Licensed Spravato® Treatment Center · Darlington, SC

Spravato® (esketamine) under attentive clinical supervision.

FDA-approved prescription nasal spray used alongside an oral antidepressant for adults with treatment-resistant depression (TRD) or major depressive disorder with acute suicidal ideation or behavior, administered strictly within a certified clinic.

How a Spravato® Visit Works

1

30-Minute Candidacy Consultation

Review treatment history, clinical eligibility, and insurance coverage verification.

2

In-Clinic Self-Administration

Administered in our private Darlington Spravato® room under clinician oversight.

3

Minimum 2-Hour Monitored Observation

Vital signs, blood pressure, and comfort are monitored until safe for discharge.

4

Arranged Ride Home

Patients may not drive or operate machinery until the next day after a restful sleep.

Eligibility & Safety Considerations

Spravato® is not appropriate for everyone. It is contraindicated in patients with aneurysmal vascular disease, arteriovenous malformation, history of intracerebral hemorrhage, or hypersensitivity to esketamine/ketamine.

Common temporary side effects include dissociation, dizziness, nausea, sedation, vertigo, increased blood pressure, and anxiety. Because of sedation and dissociation risks, Spravato® is available only through the FDA REMS program. Individual outcomes vary and results are never guaranteed.

Insurance & Prior Authorization

Certain health insurance plans cover Spravato® when clinical criteria are met. Coverage, copays, and prior authorization requirements vary by plan.

Patients should verify their benefits with their insurer, and our office will discuss documentation steps during your 30-minute consultation.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need a consultation before starting Spravato®? +

Yes. A'NuCare offers a 30-minute consultation to review your medical history, prior antidepressants, and determine whether you are an appropriate candidate.

Can I drive myself home after a session? +

No. For your safety under REMS guidelines, you must arrange transportation home and refrain from driving or operating heavy machinery until the following day after a full night's sleep.
